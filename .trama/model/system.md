# LARP

A macOS app for Apple silicon Macs that records the user's calls and transcribes them on the Mac, without sending audio anywhere. It lives in the menu bar, offers to record when a call app starts using the microphone, transcribes the user's voice and the other participants in Italian or English, then tells the participants apart. A window keeps the archive of recorded calls, to search, read, listen to, rename, export and delete them.
