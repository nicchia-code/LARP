# macOS

The operating system of the Mac, which tells the app what happens around it.

## Events

### APP_LAUNCHED
The app has been launched, at login or by the user.

### CALL_APP_MIC_STARTED
A known call app has started using the microphone.

### CALL_APP_MIC_STOPPED
A known call app has stopped using the microphone.

### LANGUAGE_INSTALLED
The speech model for the chosen transcription language is installed on the Mac.

### LANGUAGE_DOWNLOAD_FAILED
The download of the speech model for the chosen transcription language has failed.

### NETWORK_AVAILABLE
The Mac is connected to the internet again.

### AUDIO_DEVICES_CHANGED
The microphone or the audio output of the Mac has changed, for example because headphones were plugged in or unplugged.
