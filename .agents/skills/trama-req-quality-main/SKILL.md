# Requirements: Quality, Quality

Generated by Trama from `.trama/requirements/quality/main.md`. Do not edit this file: change the Requirements through a Trama Proposal (or ask the developer).

These are the developer's Requirements for how the System is built. Follow them when you write code. The Model (`.trama/model/`) says what the System does; when a Requirement and the Model disagree, stop and tell the developer.
A section titled with a Model reference (such as `order/Paid`) applies to that element and to everything inside it.

## General

- Privacy: audio and transcripts never leave the Mac; the only network use is the download of Apple speech models and FluidAudio models.
- On an M1 Mac, recording with live transcription of both tracks must not drop audio during a one-hour call.

## call/Recording

- Audio is flushed to disk at least every 5 seconds, so a crash or power loss loses at most the last 5 seconds.
- At launch, any call still in Recording is closed at its last saved audio before anything else happens.
