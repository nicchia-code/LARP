# Shortcuts

The two global keyboard shortcuts of LARP, which work whatever app is in front: one starts or stops recording (accepting the proposal when one is shown), the other adds a bookmark to the call being recorded or to the call being listened to. Each has a key combination or none; they start as ⌥⌘R and ⌥⌘B.

## States

### Set (initial)
The shortcuts that have a combination are registered with macOS and listed in the settings with their keys.
- `user.CHANGE_SHORTCUT` → Set: when the combination is not the one of the other shortcut and macOS accepts it; it is saved and replaces the previous one.
- `user.CHANGE_SHORTCUT` → Set: when the combination is the one of the other shortcut or macOS refuses it; the shortcut keeps its previous combination and the settings say why.
- `user.CLEAR_SHORTCUT` → Set: the shortcut has no combination any more; the choice is saved.
