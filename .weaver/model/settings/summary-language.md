# Summary language

The language in which omp writes the summaries: the transcription language of each call, Italian or English. A new choice applies to summaries written after it; a summary already written keeps its language.

## States

### Chosen (initial)
The choice is "Come la call" unless another one was saved.
- `user.CHOOSE_SUMMARY_LANGUAGE` → Chosen: "Come la call", "Italiano" or "English"; the choice is saved.
