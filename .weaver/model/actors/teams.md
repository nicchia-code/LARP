# Microsoft Teams

The Microsoft Teams app on the Mac, as LARP reads it through the Accessibility of macOS while Teams access is allowed. Teams does not know it is read; what it shows can change with every version of Teams, so each of its Events may simply never come.

## Events

### MEETING_JOINED
Teams shows a meeting the user has joined, with the title of the meeting when it can be read.

### MEETING_LEFT
Teams no longer shows a meeting the user is in.

### PARTICIPANTS_READ
Teams shows the names of the people in the meeting, for example in its list of participants.

### SPEAKER_CHANGED
Teams marks another person of the meeting as speaking, with the name, or marks nobody.
