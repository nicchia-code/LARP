# Transcription

What happens to a recorded call: its audio is kept, it is transcribed on the Mac with the user's voice as "Io" and everybody else as "Altri", then the other participants are told apart by their voices and, when the user wants it, omp refines the text and writes a summary of the call. The user can rename a call and its participants, mark moments of it with bookmarks, keep notes on it, and delete it.
