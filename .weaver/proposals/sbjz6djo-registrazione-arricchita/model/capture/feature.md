# Capture

Noticing that a watched call app has started using the microphone, offering to record the call or recording it on its own, and starting, pausing and stopping recordings by hand, from the proposal or with the global shortcut, from the popover of the menu bar icon, the proposal panel under it, the recording pill, the live window or the main window. While a recording runs, the chosen microphone (the user's voice) and the system audio (everything the Mac plays except the app itself, that is the other participants) are captured as two tracks on the Mac, and a pill or the live window shows it. While the system audio is captured, the sound of the other participants that the microphone picks up from the speakers is removed from the microphone. Both captures follow the audio devices of the Mac as they change, and the user is told with small notices under the menu bar icon when the microphone or the system audio is lost, when the system audio is not available or when the sound of the others cannot be removed.
