# Library

The main window of LARP, with its dark sidebar and one page at a time: the library of the recorded and imported calls as a grid of cards with their waveforms, where the user finds a call by searching and filtering by call app, groups calls into folders and imports audio files; the list of every bookmark; the page of one call, where the user reads its summary, notes and bookmarks beside its transcript, finds words in the transcript and corrects its text, listens to its audio at the speed chosen while the transcript follows, renames the call and its participants, copies its summary or transcript, exports it as a Markdown file and deletes it; and the settings. Refinement, restoring, summaries and corrections are asked from the page of a call, but carried out by the call.
