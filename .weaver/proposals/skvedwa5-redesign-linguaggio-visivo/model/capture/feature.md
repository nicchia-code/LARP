# Capture

Noticing that a known call app has started using the microphone, offering to record the call, and starting and stopping recordings by hand or from the proposal, from the popover of the menu bar icon, the proposal panel under it or the main window. While a recording runs, the microphone (the user's voice) and the system audio (everything the Mac plays except the app itself, that is the other participants) are captured as two tracks on the Mac. While the system audio is captured, the sound of the other participants that the microphone picks up from the speakers is removed from the microphone. Both captures follow the audio devices of the Mac as they change, and the user is told with small notices under the menu bar icon when the microphone or the system audio is lost, when the system audio is not available or when the sound of the others cannot be removed.
