# Library

The main window of LARP, with its dark sidebar and one page at a time: the library of the recorded calls as a grid of cards, where the user finds a call by searching; the page of one call, where the user reads its summary beside its transcript, listens to its audio while the transcript follows, renames the call and its participants, exports it as a Markdown file and deletes it; and the settings. Refinement, restoring and summaries are asked from the page of a call, but carried out by the call.
