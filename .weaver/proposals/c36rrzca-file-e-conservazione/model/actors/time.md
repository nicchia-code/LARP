# Time

The passing of time, for the waits the app sets itself.

## Events

### TRANSCRIPTION_RETRY_DUE
One minute has passed since the transcription of a call from its saved audio failed.

### ACCESSIBILITY_CHECK_DUE
Two seconds have passed since the Accessibility permission was last read while LARP waits for the user to grant it.

### AUDIO_RETENTION_CHECK_DUE
An hour has passed since the audio of the calls was last checked against the retention chosen.
