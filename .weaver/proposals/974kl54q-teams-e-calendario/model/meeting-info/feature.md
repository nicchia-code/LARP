# Meeting information

What LARP learns about a meeting from outside its audio, on the Mac and only when the user allows it: from Microsoft Teams, read through the Accessibility of macOS, the title of the meeting, the people in it and who is speaking; from the calendar of the Mac, the event in progress with its title and its invited people, and the next meetings of the day. It gives titles to calls, names to their participants and the list of the next meetings in the popover; none of it is ever sent to omp.
