# Main window, library and call page

## main-window
- **Window**: about 1200 × 920 px when first opened, at least 900 × 600; no visible title bar: the window buttons sit on the dark sidebar, the content reaches the top edge, and the window has no toolbar.
- **Sidebar**: 210 px wide, ink, full height, 16 px top and 14 px side padding, 24 px between its groups; the LARP mark 30 px with "LARP"; "Nuova registrazione" as a full-width lime button 40 px high with ink text and the shortcut on its right in JetBrains Mono, disabled (40 % opacity) while something is proposed or recorded; then the items "Tutte le call" and "Segnalibri", 13 px `#E9EAE3`, 8 × 10 px padding, radius 8, the selected one `#23251F` with lime semibold text; then the eyebrow "Cartelle" in JetBrains Mono `#A3A69B`, the folders as items, and "+ Nuova cartella" in `#A3A69B`, which turns into a text field on dark while the name is typed (Return creates, Esc cancels); at the bottom, while work runs, a 7 px lime dot with "Trascrizione sul Mac · <n>" in 12 px `#A3A69B`, then "Impostazioni".
- The sidebar item "Azioni" sits between "Tutte le call" and "Segnalibri" and is drawn like them.
- **Library pages**: paper background, 28 × 36 px padding, 24 px between blocks.
  - header: the eyebrow "<day short name> <day> <month short name> · <n> call oggi", the title of the view in Bricolage Grotesque 44 px, and on the right a search field 300 px wide with the placeholder "Cerca parole, persone, decisioni";
  - banner of the proposal, ink, radius 18, 20 × 22 px padding: the eyebrow with the red dot "In onda ora · <call app>", the title of the proposal card in Bricolage Grotesque 24 px with the number of people under it in 13 px `#A3A69B` when known, a still decorative row of `#3A3C36` bars in the middle, and on the right the text button "Non questa" in `#E9EAE3` and the lime button "Registra questa call" with an ink dot;
  - **filter row**, above the cards of all calls and of a folder: the filter chips (32 px, the selected one ink with lime text) and, on the right, the underlined text button "Importa file audio" in 12 px semibold;
  - **all calls** and **folder**: a grid of cards, at least 280 px each and as many columns as fit, 16 px gaps. A card is a white card with 14 px between its rows: eyebrow with day and time on the left and the call app on the right; the title in Bricolage Grotesque 19 px, at most two lines; the waveform in an ink strip 44 px high, radius 10, 10 px side padding, with the 44 bars coloured by speaker (flex width, at most 4 px each, 2 px gaps), or `#6B6E64` bars for a call without lines, the strip left out while the waveform is not worked out; the summary line or status in 13 px `#5A5D55`, at most two lines, at least two lines high; a row with the avatars of the participants ("Io" included), the duration in 12 px `#5A5D55` and, on the right, the status chip: lime for a call whose transcript is complete, `#ECECE4` with "…" texts while it is being worked on;
  - **bookmarks view**: one white card per call with bookmarks, its eyebrow with day, time and call app and its title in Bricolage Grotesque 19 px, then one row per bookmark: the time in a JetBrains Mono chip `#ECECE4` and the line text in 13 px, at most two lines; a row highlights `#F1F1EA` under the pointer;
  - **actions view**: one white card per call with action items, its eyebrow with day, time and call app and its title in Bricolage Grotesque 19 px, then one row per item as on the call page (tick, text, owner avatar and name, due), the done ones struck through in `#5A5D55` on `#F1F1EA`; a click outside the tick opens the call;
  - the empty texts sit centred in the content area in 13 px `#5A5D55`.
- **Call page**: paper background; 28 × 32 px padding.
  - top row: "‹ Archivio" as a 13 px link on the left; on the right the outline buttons "Sposta in cartella" (with the name of the current folder when there is one) and "Copia sintesi", the dark button "Esporta" with lime text, and an outline button "…" opening the menu of the call; "Copiata" replaces the label of a copy button for two seconds;
  - the eyebrow "<call app> · <day and time> · <duration>", then the title in Bricolage Grotesque 56 px (editable in place, same font), then the status of the call in 13 px `#5A5D55`, then the row of speaker chips (avatar, name and the share in JetBrains Mono 11 px `#5A5D55`, 32 px high, radius 16, white with a `#E4E4DC` border) and the underlined link "Rinomina parlanti", which puts the first participant chip into renaming; clicking a participant chip renames it; the rename field lists the names offered below it, as chips to click, filtered by what is typed;
  - two columns, about 55 % and 45 %, 24 px apart. Left: the tabs "Sintesi", "Le mie note", "Segnalibri" in Bricolage Grotesque 18 px, the selected one ink with a 3 px lime underline and the others `#5A5D55`, over a 1 px ink line; "Sintetizza" or "Rigenera" as an outline button on the right of the tabs while the summary is shown.
    - Summary: the opening sentence in Inter 24 px medium, line height 1.3, −0.01 em, with its key part on a lime `#D4F24A` background, radius 6, padding 0 4 px; then the sections, each with a JetBrains Mono eyebrow title: "Decisioni" as rows separated by `#E4E4DC` dividers with the number "01", "02" in Bricolage Grotesque 20 px, the text in 15 px and on the right the time in a JetBrains Mono chip `#ECECE4` that plays from it; "Azioni" as rows in white cards, radius 10, `#E4E4DC` border, each with an 18 px square tick (ink filled with a white check when done), the text in 13 px (struck through in `#5A5D55` on `#F1F1EA` when done), and on the right the owner's avatar with "<name> · <due>" in 12 px `#5A5D55`; "Rimasto aperto", "Concetti chiave" and "Altro" as 15 px text; without a summary the state of the summary in 15 px `#5A5D55`.
    - Notes: one row per note, its time in a JetBrains Mono chip `#ECECE4` (none without time), its text in 15 px editable in place, a delete button that appears under the pointer; at the bottom a white field "Scrivi una nota…" that adds the note with Return.
    - Bookmarks: one row per bookmark, its time in a JetBrains Mono chip, the line text in 15 px, a delete button under the pointer.
    - Under the tabs, at the bottom of the left column: the questions and answers, each question in 13 px semibold and its answer in 15 px under it, the last one with "omp sta rispondendo…" in `#5A5D55` while it waits, a failed one with "Riprova" as a link; then the ink field "Chiedi qualcosa a questa call", 52 px high, radius 26, with the placeholder in `#A3A69B` and the lime button "Chiedi" inside it on the right, disabled while omp is answering.
  - Right: the transcript panel, a white card with the heading "Trascrizione" in Bricolage Grotesque 20 px and, on its right, the field "Trova una parola" (`#F1F1EA`, radius 16, 32 px high, with a magnifying glass, the count "<n> di <m>" and up and down arrows while text is typed); each line is the dot of its speaker colour and the eyebrow "<SPEAKER> <time>" over the text in 13 px, with "⌑ SEGNALIBRO" in JetBrains Mono at the right of the eyebrow of a bookmarked line; the line being heard has a `#EEF8B4` background, radius 10; the found words have a lime background and the found line being looked at a 2 px ink outline; while "Modifica testo" is on, each text is a borderless editable field with a `#E4E4DC` underline and the link reads "Fine"; at the bottom of the panel the underlined 12 px links "Modifica testo" and "Copia trascrizione";
  - in the transcript panel the link "Traduci" sits between "Modifica testo" and "Copia trascrizione"; it reads "Traduzione in corso…" while omp translates and "Mostra originale" while the translation is shown, with the eyebrow "TRADOTTO IN <LANGUAGE>" in JetBrains Mono `#5A5D55` under the heading of the panel; a failed translation shows "Traduzione non riuscita" in `#B42A18` next to the link;
  - below the columns, the playback bar across the page.

## playback
- **Playback bar**: ink, radius 18, 64 px high, 14 px padding: a 48 px round lime play/pause button with an ink glyph, the time reached in JetBrains Mono white, the timeline, the length of the audio in JetBrains Mono `#A3A69B`, a round 36 px "Segnalibro" button on `#2A2C26` that marks the time reached, the speed as a 32 px chip with a `#3A3C36` border and white JetBrains Mono text ("1,0×", "1,25×"…), and the chip "Salta i silenzi", lime outline and lime text when on, `#3A3C36` outline and `#A3A69B` text when off.
- **Timeline**: one segment per transcript line placed at its time, coloured with its speaker colour, radius 4, 2 px gaps; the parts already heard are full colour and the rest at 45 % opacity; a 2 px white bar marks the time reached; small lime ticks above the timeline mark the bookmarks; clicking or dragging on the timeline moves the position.
- Without audio the bar shows "Audio non disponibile" in `#A3A69B` in place of the timeline, with the play and bookmark buttons disabled.
